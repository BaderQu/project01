Why I Am Learning to Code

Coding is the skill that lets me turn ideas into real, working tools. This page explains what drives me to learn it.

My Motivation

I studied cybersecurity and cloud computing, and I quickly realized that understanding how software is built is the best way to understand how it can be broken or protected.

Writing code also gives me a way to solve problems on my own instead of depending on ready-made tools.

Curiosity

I enjoy asking how things work and then testing the answer myself.
Every small project teaches me something new.

My Goals

My goal is to become a skilled and reliable engineer who can build secure and well-structured applications.

Next Steps

I want to master the web fundamentals first,
then move on to scripting, automation, and cloud projects.
